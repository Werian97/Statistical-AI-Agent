This dataset contains 4,600 historical property sales records, primarily focused on the
Washington State real estate market (including Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, and surrounding
areas). It provides a comprehensive overview of how structural, historical, and geographic
features impact property valuation.
There are some missing values filled with "0" or "0.0".
For every field is specified if it is "Numerical", which means it has only numerical values.
If a field is Numerical it is specified if it is "Fractional" which means all numbers has fractional component (eventually ".0")
For example: price is Numerical - Fractional, therefore it cannot contain a value such as "300000",
it would be written as "300000.0".

The fields are:
- date: The specific date when the real estate transaction was recorded (Format: YYYY-MM-DD).
- price: (Numerical - Fractional) The final sale price of the property in USD (Target Variable).
- bedrooms: (Numerical - Fractional) The total number of bedrooms in the property.
- bathrooms: (Numerical - Fractional) The total number of bathrooms. Fractional values (e.g., 1.5, 2.25) indicate partial baths (e.g., a toilet and sink but no shower).
- sqft_living: (Numerical) The total interior square footage of the living space.
- sqft_lot: (Numerical) The total square footage of the land or lot the property sits on.
- floors: (Numerical - Fractional) The number of stories or levels in the property.
- waterfront: (Numerical) A binary indicator (1 or 0) denoting whether the property has a waterfront.
- view: (Numerical) A rated index (0 to 4) indicating the overall quality of the property's view.
- condition: (Numerical) A rated index (1 to 5) indicating the overall structural and aesthetic condition of the property.
- sqft_above: (Numerical) Square footage of the house apart from basement.
- sqft_basement: (Numerical) Square footage of the basement.
- yr_built: (Numerical) The year when the house was built.
- yr_renovated: (Numerical) The year when the house was renovated.
- street: The address of the house.
- city: The city where the house is.
- statezip: ZIP code relative to the house.
- price_per_sqft: (Numerical - Fractional) price per square foot.
